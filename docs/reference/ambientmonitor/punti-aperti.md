# Open points

A list of what the engine does not do yet or has not verified. The original plan (phases 0-5: Martian environment, electrical bus, thermal control) was implemented on 15 October 2026 and deleted; it remains in the git history (`git log -- docs/plan.md`). The sources of the numerical values are in [`verifica-dati.md`](verifica-dati.md).

## Physical engine

1. **Life-support priority (implemented, reserve insufficient in a strong storm).** The bus has three levels (`lifeSupport` > `essential` > `modulable`); scrubber, O₂ generator, fans and pump are `lifeSupport` and drain the battery down to 0, the other loads stop at `lifeReserveSoc` = 0.22 (ESTIMATE with no NASA source: ~12 h of 0.75 kW on 40 kWh, see `verifica-dati.md` §5). Strong storm (τ = 6 for 2 sols, then τ = 0.5, 64 h, `initialSoc` 0.12), before → after: ppCO₂ max 9.02 → 8.62 mmHg; PIO₂ min 106.9 → 113.2 mmHg; minimum temperature 0.68 → 0.13 °C; minimum SoC 0 → 0 (life support was not served in full for 34 h, from the 14th hour); mass residual ~3e-11 kg, energy 270 → 373 J. The improvement is small: the battery starts below the reserve (0.12 < 0.22) and with τ = 6 production does not cover 0.75 kW. More battery or panels are needed, or `source: "fixed"`; the reserve alone is not enough. The minimum temperature worsens a little because the non-emergency heaters are cut first.
   **O₂ emergency mode (16 October 2026).** With the bus at `lifeSupportOnly` the O₂ generator aims at `emergencySetpointKPa` (18.15 kPa, PIO₂ ≈ 124 mmHg) instead of 18.63 kPa. Storm τ = 6 for 3 sols, `initialSoc` 0.6, current scene, before → after: battery at the end of sol 1 0% → 3.9%; sol-1 consumption 34.1 → 32.7 kWh (−1.4 kWh); the battery runs out during sol 2 instead of sol 1 (~1.5-2 h more). From sol 3 identical: production (12.1 kWh/sol, 0.5 kW mean) does not cover even life support alone (0.75 kW = 18 kWh/sol), so no software saves it: absolute PIO₂, T and RH stay in alarm for the whole storm. Levers still open: more battery or panels, a crew with reduced metabolism (point 6: electrolysis weighs ~10.6 kWh/sol, 59% of life support), emergency heaters below 16 °C at `lifeSupport` priority with a power cap.
2. **Oxygen alarms (implemented).** `pio2_1h` (1 h mean < 127 mmHg), `pio2_abs` (< 122 mmHg), `pio2_hyper` (> 356 mmHg, warning) on PIO₂ = (PB − 47)·FO₂ [S20]. In the storm run they are active at the end of the run (PIO₂ 121 mmHg). With the design atmosphere the margin on 127 is ~2 mmHg: 1 h of normal RH or ppO₂ drift could trigger the alarm; to be reconfirmed on long runs (`test:long` not rerun).
3. **Dust tied to τ.** The deposit on panels and radiator does not depend on the storm; cleaning happens every 30 sols and brings the factor back to 0.95, with no threshold. Deposit proportional to τ and threshold cleaning are needed, with the rate taken from a source ([S13] is already in the sources document).
4. **Temperature in a storm.** The exterior profile does not reduce the temperature swing with τ nor warm the sky. Sources are needed (REMS, MEDA).
5. **Long-term energy deficit (solved with a clear sky, open in a storm).** Before 16 October 2026, with 55 m² of panels and 40 kWh of battery, the SoC at the start of the sol fell from 61% to 49% in 9 sols and reached zero in ~20 sols. After the thermal fixes (heat pump, dehum, external loop capacity) and with the new loads, 10 sols of clear sky: consumption 37.3–38.6 kWh/sol, production 48.0 → 47.2 kWh/sol (dust 0.002/sol), SoC at the start of the sol 42.1–42.9% with no drift from sol 2 (the surplus is discarded when the battery is full). Energy residual ~150–220 J/sol, mass up to 1e-10 kg at sol 10 (grows slowly: from 5e-12 at sol 1). With τ = 3 the battery reaches 0 in sol 2 (4 sols, consumption 24 kWh/sol equal to production from sol 3, in an energy blackout: RH 100%, ppCO₂ up to 8 mmHg); with τ = 6 in sol 1 (in sol 2 with emergency mode). The plant remains to be resized, or `power.source: "fixed"` used for storms.
6. **Crew response to the emergency.** In a storm the crew does not change activity; it could reduce it to consume less O₂ and produce less CO₂.
7. **Condensation on the walls.** With RH ~98% and cold walls condensation is not modelled.
8. **Metabolism at 70 kPa.** The BVAD data hold at 101.3 kPa; at 70 kPa the latent share grows (not modelled).
9. **Internal `hIn`** not corrected for gravity and pressure (see `verifica-dati.md` §1.1).
10. **Lighting, water treatment, galley** are only generic loads in `power.loads` (`galley`, `lighting`, `water`, with `schedule` in LTST hours). The galley (960 W) comes from BVAD [S2] §4.4.6, but duration and time are estimates; lights (150 W) and water (200 W) are estimates with no primary source (see `verifica-dati.md` §2.4). NASA values are still missing for the power of the OGA, scrubber (CDRA/4BMS), the wall U and IT/communications loads.
10b. **Ground warmer than the air at night.** At steady state (45 sols, environment only) the ground stays 14–15 K above the air at night (ground 196–280 K, air 184–242 K); at Gale the REMS data normally show the opposite inversion (air at 1.5 m warmer than the ground at night) [Viúdez-Moreiras et al. 2019, JGR]. It is not a start-up artefact: the ground's T0 does not matter after a few sols. The lever is `exterior.skyDeltaK` (estimate, 25 K): with 40 K the night difference drops to 9 K, with 60 K to 3 K (TI 300). A sourced value (mean ground−air ΔT from REMS/PDS) is missing to choose it; it was not changed. The air profile is the VL1 preset (Viking), not Gale. Effect on the model: the radiator sees a ground that is too warm (Fg = 0.5) and rejects less than the real amount at night.
11. **External profiles.** `exterior.table` is supported but no PDS data is included; Gale and Jezero require `minK`/`maxK` or a table.

## Tests and checks

12. **Long pressure test (3 sols)** not rerun after the introduction of the overpressure valve; it should pass.
13. **Long test with τ = 3:** the assertion `minSocSeen >= 0` failed, probably because of floating-point rounding in the battery discharge (not verified). The bus now limits the discharge to 0 with `Math.max(0, …)`, so the assertion should hold; not rerun (`test:long`).
14. **South zone:** `|T − setpoint|` < 1 K for 89% of the time against the 90% required (exercises near the probe weigh on it).
15. **Speed:** 292× in the 10-sol run, below the 300× required by the long test.
16. **Real data.** No PDS data (REMS, MEDA) compared with the model. To be tuned by hand, they remain estimates: `uWall`, `skyDeltaK`, `cb`, the CHX UA, radiator, panel and battery sizes, the valve's maximum flow (`reliefKgS`).

## Calibration notes (for whoever edits thermal/power)

- Table f(z,τ,al) in `engine/climate/data/appelbaum-f.js`: digitized with OCR from the NTRS TM-103623 PDF and repaired against the polynomial of Table III. The polynomial is wrong by up to 30% at z ≥ 75° and τ > 3.5, not the table.
- BVAD: 0.84 kg O₂ / 1.01 kg CO₂ per day correspond to a day without resistive exercise; with 1 h of resistive you get the long-mission values (0.90 / 1.08 kg, Table 4-1). Both are tested.
- Buoyancy: the projection uses isotropic conductances (with the geometric ones the circulation was cancelled); `cb` = 0.9 s tuned on 0.04–0.09 m/s per 1 K; the computation runs every 30 s to stay above 300×.
- Thermal control: cascade cooling demand → loop setpoint → valve PI; CHX on F1 and F2; dehumidification with a cold loop at RH > 58% (switches off below 48%); supply reheat (min 17 °C); setpoint ramp 3 K/h; zone heater below 16 °C is an essential load.
- ~54 min night cycle (fixed on 16 October 2026): dehumidification brought the loop setpoint to 7 °C, the valve dumped ~3 kW to the radiator, the loop fell below `heatPump.minLoopK` (283 K) and the heat pump locked out, leaving heaters and reheat ~2 kW electrical. With `minLoopK` = 275 K the pump stays on (on/off cycles from 63 to ~0 per sol) and a sol's consumption (without the new loads) falls from 43.0 to 32.2 kWh, with dehum thresholds 58/48 and `external.capJK` 120 kJ/K; T deviation from the setpoint unchanged (N 0.45 K, S 0.71 K rms). Estimates: `minLoopK` and `capJK`.
- Sizing: radiator 12 m² (with 8 m² the cabin rises to 23-25 °C by day), panels 55 m², battery 40 kWh, heat pump active (−10-14% consumption, and without it the battery empties in 2 sols).
- CHX: mean surface temperature = `T_loop + Q/UA_water`; with the estimate on the air side the CHX did not condense.
- Sensitivity (1 sol): `uWall` 0.2 brings consumption to 60.7 kWh, the battery to zero and the cabin to 7.5 °C; `skyDeltaK` 15/25/40 K changes consumption by ±5%.
- Tightness target: mass balance ~1e-12 kg, energy ~300 J/sol.
